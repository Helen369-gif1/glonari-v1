    What Gia can and cannot do

    Can Gia act without asking me?

    Not for anything important. She can research, summarize and draft on her own. Sending, sharing, booking or paying always waits for your approval.

    What does Gia remember?

    Only what you choose to save. You can review, edit or remove any item in Memory at any time.

    What happens when Gia isn't sure?

    She tells you what she couldn't verify and suggests how to check it, instead of guessing.

    When does Gia hand off to a person?

    When a request needs a licensed professional or a human expert. She says so and helps you prepare.

    Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

    Answer to come from the product and legal teams.
