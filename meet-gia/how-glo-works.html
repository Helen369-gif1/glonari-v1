How GLO works

Capacity you can direct where it’s needed.

Use it with your Gia, put it to work on an authorized project, or transfer GLO itself where available. After a project ends, capacity may become available again.

See the three-layer model Compare your options

The three-layer model

Direction. Intelligence. Capacity.

Three roles that should never be collapsed into one. One example task runs through all three: a briefing on three markets, due Thursday.

  1. YouDirection

    You provide goals, preferences, requirements, approvals and Human Intelligence™.

    In the exampleThree markets, public sources only, ready by Thursday. You approve the extra research.

  2. GiaIntelligence & orchestration

    Gia understands the task, plans the work, selects resources, coordinates execution and keeps you informed.

    In the examplePlans the research, picks the models and agents, compares the markets and brings back a draft with its sources.

  3. GLOCapacity & access

    Qualifying GLO can provide access to additional Glonari resources and capabilities under applicable program terms.

    In the exampleMakes qualifying research and compute capacity available for the longer task.

GLO isn’t the intelligence. Gia is.

Reusable capacity

The project can end without the GLO ending.

When a qualifying project is completed and its requirements are satisfied, the participating member continues to hold the GLO. Subject to applicable conditions, that capacity may later become available again for another qualifying use.

You hold your GLO the whole time

Capacity may become available again; it does not reset automatically and is not always reusable. Future project availability, utilization and compensation are not guaranteed.

Global Connections vs. secondary market

Two different mechanisms. Do not mix them.

Global Connections®

The work moves. The GLO doesn’t.

  1. You hold GLO.
  2. You review and authorize a qualifying project.
  3. Gia performs the work using available qualifying capacity.
  4. The deliverable returns to the requesting member, who pays for the work under the project terms.
  5. You receive project compensation under its terms.
  6. You keep your GLO.

Deliverable returned · GLO retained

Independent secondary market

The GLO moves. Ownership changes.

  1. You decide to transfer GLO.
  2. The transaction takes place on an independent decentralized exchange, where available.
  3. Another participant acquires the GLO and becomes the new holder.
  4. The market sets the price.

GLO transferred · ownership changed

Important limit

Glonari does not operate the decentralized exchange, match buyers and sellers, act as buyer or seller, hold GLO in custody for the transaction or set the market price. Liquidity and resale are not guaranteed.

Final call to action & important information

Understand the mechanism before choosing a path.

GLO is a capacity and access layer that Gia can use within qualifying programs. What is available can vary by member, program, project, jurisdiction, market conditions and technical availability.

Important information

  • Examples are illustrative.
  • Opportunities, GLO requirements, duration, compensation, participation, availability and results vary.
  • Compensation is earned through work under applicable project terms.
  • GLO itself does not pay income, interest or yield.
  • GLO does not guarantee appreciation, liquidity or resale.
  • Independent secondary transactions are subject to applicable law, market conditions, technical availability and third-party terms.
